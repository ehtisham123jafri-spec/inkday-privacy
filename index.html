Inkday

One line. Every day.

Privacy Policy · Last updated: October 1, 2026

Summary

Inkday is a private, one-sentence daily journal. Everything you write stays on your device. We do not run servers for the app, we do not have user accounts, and we do not collect, sell or share your personal information.

1. Information we collect

We do not collect any personal information. The lines you write, the moods you choose, your streak and your settings (theme, reminder time) are stored only in the app's private storage on your device. They are never sent to us or to any third party by the app.

2. Information we do not collect

  • No name, email address, phone number or account details
  • No location data
  • No contacts, photos, files, microphone or camera access
  • No advertising ID, analytics or crash-tracking data

3. Permissions the app uses

  • Notifications: used only to show your optional daily reminder. The reminder is scheduled locally on your device. You can turn it off at any time in the app's Settings or in your phone's system settings.
  • Internet: used only to download the app's text fonts from Google Fonts. See the next section.
  • Receive boot completed: used only so your reminder keeps working after you restart your phone.

4. Third-party services

Inkday downloads its fonts (Bricolage Grotesque and Literata) from Google Fonts. When the fonts are first downloaded, your device contacts Google's servers, and Google may receive technical information that any web request includes, such as your IP address. No journal content or personal data from the app is included in these requests. Google's handling of that information is described in the Google Privacy Policy. Inkday contains no advertising networks, analytics services or social-media SDKs.

5. Your data and your control

  • You can edit or delete any single line from the Calendar or Memories screens.
  • You can delete all lines at once with "Delete all my lines" in Settings.
  • Uninstalling the app removes its locally stored data from your device.

Because we never receive your data, we cannot access, recover or delete it on your behalf.

6. Device backups

Your phone's operating system may include app data in its own device backup (for example, Android's backup to your Google account), depending on your device settings. That backup is controlled by you and your device provider, not by Inkday. You can manage or turn off backups in your phone's system settings.

7. Data security

Your entries are kept in the app's private storage, which other apps cannot normally access. No method of storing data is perfectly secure, so we recommend using your phone's screen lock to protect your device.

8. Children's privacy

Inkday is not directed to children under 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from anyone, including children.

9. Changes to this policy

If we change this policy, we will update the "Last updated" date at the top of this page. Significant changes will also be noted in the app's store listing or release notes.
